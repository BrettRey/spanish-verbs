## ts-fsrs (phone app)

The phone app uses the maintained `ts-fsrs` 5.4.2 package from https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs (MIT), rather than the Python port. Its complete licence is copied from the installed package into `web/dist/data/LICENSE-FSRS.txt` at build time.
